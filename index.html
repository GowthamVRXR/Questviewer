Real-time video and audio

Hardware H.264/HEVC on the headset, Opus audio, carried by WebRTC straight to your PC. Typically well under 100 ms on a local network — close enough to coach someone through what they are doing while they do it.

Zero-configuration discovery

Put a headset on the same Wi-Fi and it appears. No IP addresses, no pairing codes, no router changes, nothing to configure on the headset itself.

Live quality control

Change resolution, frame rate and bitrate per headset while it streams. No restart.

Sessions that heal

Questions

The things people ask first.

Can I use it to record Beat Saber or other store games?

No. Quest Viewer only shows applications built with the Quest Streaming SDK — software you or your developer built and deployed. It cannot capture other Quest apps, store-bought games, or the system menus. There is no covert mode and no way to add one. If you need to capture a game you did not build, a capture card is the right tool.

How much code do I have to write?

None. You import the package, run two wizard items from the Unity menu bar, and build as usual. There are no SDK calls to make, no prefabs to wire and no callbacks to implement. Spectator views, recording and multi-headset viewing are all driven from the receiver.

Does the SDK phone home or check a licence?

No. The SDK is free, ships with complete C# source, contains no activation check and makes no call to us. You can read every line before you ship it. The paid licence applies only to the Windows receiver, and only above the free tier's limits.

What happens when a paid licence expires?

The app returns to the free tier — one headset, 720p. Nothing is deleted, nothing is locked, and the version you installed keeps working forever. A term only covers releases published during it.

We have ten headsets on one Wi-Fi. Will that hold up?

The architecture is built for it, and the receiver is tested against a ten-headset target. The practical limit is your access point, not the software — ten simultaneous 1080p streams is real bandwidth. Drop the per-headset quality from the PC and it stays smooth on modest hardware. Every headset is an independent peer connection, so one dropping out does not affect the rest.

Can I move a licence to a different computer?

Yes, as often as you like. The licence window lists every computer the key is active on and lets you release any of them — including machines you no longer have access to, which matters after a Windows reinstall.

Does any video reach your servers?

It cannot. Video travels directly between headset and PC over WebRTC. We operate no media servers, so there is nothing for it to pass through. Only licence activation contacts our payment provider, and that sends the key, your computer's name and a short hash — nothing else.
