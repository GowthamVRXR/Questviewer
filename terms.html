2. What the software does

Quest Viewer receives live video streamed from Meta Quest headsets over your local network and displays it on a Windows PC.

Quest Viewer only works with VR applications built using the Quest Streaming SDK. It cannot capture, mirror or record arbitrary Quest applications, games or system screens. A headset appears in Quest Viewer only because software you or your developer built, and chose to deploy, is transmitting to it.

Video travels directly between the headset and your PC. We operate no media servers and have no technical means to see, store or record what you stream. See our Privacy Policy.
